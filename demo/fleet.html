Assumptions, provenance and the boundary of this result

This independent synthetic experiment assumes local command expiry on disconnected devices and compares example redistribution policies. It does not represent Base's architecture, operating policy or controller. Internal need and adoption are not validated.

Device ratings, energy, backup reserves, the 30 or 40 second local stop, 40 second bound, noiseless telemetry, immediate command acknowledgements and constant target are simulation assumptions. No private Base limits are known. AI is not in the control loop.

ERCOT Winter Storm Uri settlement prices remain separate historical context. They are not an outage measurement or an automatic reason to discharge. No Beryl data is used here.

Both strategies renew commands on the same 10-second cadence, enforce the same local reserve floor and start from identical state, seed and fault schedule. Baseline keeps initial equal shares. The constrained controller redistributes only after reserving the maximum possible output of unseen devices. It does not minimize device count or prove an optimal policy.

Question for future validation: What stop-time bound, acknowledgement and telemetry semantics, reserve constraints and incumbent baseline would make this experiment representative enough to judge the added complexity?
